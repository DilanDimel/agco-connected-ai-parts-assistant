The problem

Today the journey is reactive and slow

Nothing happens until the farmer notices a breakdown, which often comes during planting or harvest. After that, every step relies on phone calls and manual lookups.

As-isCurrent process (Sprint 4 model)
  1. 1Farmer notices the problemDiscovery is reactive. They may not know the part number. Downtime starts here.
  2. 2Farmer calls or drives to the dealerIdentification depends on a verbal description.
  3. 3Dealer looks up the part manuallyIf the part can't be identified, it goes back to the farmer. Rework loop.
  4. 4Dealer checks local stock, then AGCO stockTwo separate systems, checked one after the other.
  5. 5Farmer waits on a backorderUpdates only come if the dealer remembers to call. Uncertain ETA.
To-beWith the Connected AI Parts Assistant
  1. 1Tractor reports a fault code, or the farmer snaps a photoTwo ways to start, and neither needs a phone call. Proactive.
  2. 2AI suggests the genuine partUses the photo, serial number, and fault-code table to suggest a part, with a confidence score.
  3. 3One live stock checkDealer and AGCO warehouse inventory are checked together through ERP. Instant answer.
  4. 4Dealer reviews and approvesLow-confidence or costly cases always go to a human first.
  5. 5Farmer gets automatic updatesEvery date change triggers a text or email. No chasing.
The solution

Three components in one journey

Each component runs through FarmerCore, the parts app farmers already use, and the dealer's existing parts system. AGCO already owns most of the pieces. What's missing is the link between them.

Connected-tractor alerts

If the farmer opts in, AGCO Connect tractors send fault codes to the assistant. It maps each code to the likely part and checks stock before the farmer calls.

Solves: reactive discovery and peak-season downtime. Benchmark: John Deere Connected Support.
Human in the loop

Dealers stay in control

The AI never commits money or changes a repair plan by itself. In the prototype this shows up as the 80% confidence rule. Anything below 80% goes to a parts specialist.

AI acts alone
Low risk · logged and reviewed weekly

Order-status answers from live data, backorder date-change alerts, and catalog results.

AI recommends
Dealer parts specialist approves

Low-confidence part matches, substitute parts, and parts suggested from a fault code.

Human only
Dealer staff, escalate to AGCO

Refunds, warranty claims, pricing exceptions, expedite fees, safety parts, and harvest-critical breakdowns.

Implementation

Start small, then scale only through the gates

These timelines are team planning assumptions, not AGCO commitments.

0
Months 1–3

Prepare

Put security controls in place first. Sign up 10–15 pilot dealers, build a fault-to-part table for the top 25 codes, and capture baseline metrics.

1
Months 4–9

Pilot one season

Run all three components with the pilot dealers. Hold weekly dealer-council reviews and log every override of an AI suggestion.

2
Months 10–18

Scale North America

Roll out to more of the 1,190 North American dealers, add AR technician support, and standardize the dealer API.

3
Months 19–30

Global + forecasting

Expand to Europe with a country-by-country GDPR review. Add AI demand forecasting in recommend-only mode.
